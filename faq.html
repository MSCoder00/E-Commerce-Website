Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to your most pressing questions about our storefront.

How long does standard shipping take?

Standard delivery takes between 3 to 5 business days across the United States, Canada, and Europe. Orders placed with Express Priority Courier arrive within 1 to 2 business days. Complimentary express delivery is unlocked automatically on all carts exceeding $50.

What is the MS STORE return and exchange policy?

We offer a 30-day, no-questions-asked return window for all unused items in their original packaging. Simply head to your My Account dashboard or contact customer support to receive a prepaid shipping label. Refunds are issued to your original payment method within 3 business days of receipt.

How do I track my active order?

After completing checkout, you will receive an Order ID (e.g. MS-849201). You can track your shipment anytime by navigating to My Account > Order History and tapping the "Track Order" button for live checkpoint progress.

Are electronic products covered by warranty?

Yes, every audio item, smart watch, and electronic accessory sold at MS STORE includes an authentic 2-Year Official Manufacturer Warranty covering hardware defects, battery performance, and internal circuitry.

Privacy & Data Security Policy

MS STORE adheres to strict privacy protection standards. Payment card numbers are never stored in plain text or shared with third parties. Your credentials are securely managed through Firebase Authentication, and personal data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
